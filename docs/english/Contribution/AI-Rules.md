# AI Rules — Artificial Intelligence Policy

## Introduction

It is important to understand that the use of Artificial Intelligence is permitted during the development of Knight Shell.

However, its use must comply with certain conditions. AI can serve as a supporting tool, but it must never replace the developer's understanding, technical judgment, or responsibility.

These rules exist to ensure that the code and documentation incorporated into the project are understandable, maintainable, and functional.

## 1. Developer Responsibility

Every change incorporated into Knight Shell remains the responsibility of the contributor submitting it.

This means that:

- If you use AI to generate, modify, or fix code, you remain responsible for the result.
- If the code contains errors, blaming AI for generating it is not an acceptable justification.
- You must understand what the code you submit does and how it interacts with the rest of the project.
- Before submitting a contribution, you must review and verify the changes you have made.

Artificial Intelligence is an assistance tool, not an entity responsible for your contributions.

**Responsibility for every modification always belongs to the person submitting it.**

## 2. AI in Documentation

The use of AI to develop documentation is permitted.

You may use it to improve writing, organize explanations, correct grammatical errors, or help you structure technical information.

However, documentation contributions generated entirely by AI, without substantial involvement from the contributor, will be rejected.

Documentation must reflect genuine work in understanding, reviewing, and preparing the material on the part of the person submitting it.

We are not looking for automatically generated documents, but rather documentation that genuinely helps people understand Knight Shell.

## 3. AI in Development

The use of AI to develop code is not prohibited either.

However, there are important limitations.

If a developer's contributions, whether AI-generated or not, repeatedly present problems such as:

- Unnecessarily complex code snippets that are difficult to maintain.
- Recurring errors that demonstrate a lack of proper review.
- Implementations that negatively affect the project's stability or organization.
- Code that the contributor themselves cannot explain or justify.

The team may reject their contributions and, if a repeated pattern of malpractice is identified, remove the contributor from the development team.

In that case, they may apply for readmission and will have to demonstrate that they have corrected the problems that led to their removal.

This measure applies to development quality and responsibility, not simply to the use of Artificial Intelligence.

## 4. Final Statement

These rules are not intended to punish or restrict those who use Artificial Intelligence.

Their purpose is to ensure that Knight Shell remains a functional, organized, and maintainable project, preventing it from becoming a collection of automatically generated implementations without technical understanding or control.

**You can use AI to develop, but you must never let it develop on your behalf without knowing what you are incorporating.**

The quality of Knight Shell depends on the responsibility of those who contribute to it.
