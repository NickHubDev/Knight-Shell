# CONTRIBUTION GUIDE

### A Welcome Letter for Developers and Contributors

First of all, thank you for taking the time to contribute to **Knight Shell**.

We believe Knight Shell is more than **just a software project.** It is a ***community where developers, maintainers, and enthusiasts work together to make the shell better than it was yesterday.***

We want everyone who decides to participate to feel comfortable, respected, and welcome. Every contribution matters, whether it involves reporting a bug, suggesting an improvement, discussing an idea, or implementing a new feature.

## Where Do We Communicate?

Before diving into development, it is important to understand how communication and technical discussions are organized within Knight Shell.

Unlike projects that distribute their discussions across multiple public platforms, Knight Shell provides two dedicated spaces for communication: our **Discord Community** and **Developer Forum**.

Each platform serves a different purpose and offers a different way to interact with the project.

---

## Discord Community

### An Accessible Space for Everyone

Our Discord Community is the most accessible way to stay connected with Knight Shell.

It provides a visual and user-friendly environment where community members can exchange ideas, ask questions, share experiences, and keep up with the latest developments.

Whether you are a regular user, a beginner interested in contributing, or an experienced developer, Discord is a good place to get involved and become part of the community.

## Developer Forum

### A More Technical Environment

The Knight Shell Forum is an interactive, web-based platform designed specifically for more technical discussions.

Unlike Discord, the Forum focuses on providing a more structured environment where developers can discuss implementation details, explore new ideas, analyze technical decisions, and follow discussions involving the project's maintainers and developers.

It is not necessarily the most beginner-friendly place to start, but it is one of the most valuable resources for understanding the reasoning, discussions, and decisions behind Knight Shell's development.

**You can think of Discord as the place where the community interacts, and the Forum as the place where the project is technically discussed.**
